Pi settings & Livecraft settings — UI mockup

Two new tabs in the existing settings modal. Pi settings reads and writes ~/.pi/agent/settings.json and the workspace's .pi/settings.json with a scope switch, provenance per row, and a raw-JSON escape hatch. Livecraft exposes the browser-stored preferences and makes remembered layout state resettable. Faithful to src/features/settings/settings.css tokens and row shapes.
